Destination and journey

Final Finished Project

The finished system combines a compact 4-DOF arm, a tomato-facing perception pipeline, and learned motion policies for picking small fruit from a cluttered plant setup.

What It Does

  • Uses stereo vision to estimate where reachable cherry tomatoes are in 3D space.
  • Moves a custom arm toward the target while keeping the end effector aligned for harvesting.
  • Uses imitation learning from demonstration data to make the approach and harvest motion smoother.
  • Brings together mechanical design, perception, control, and evaluation in one physical robot.

Build Notes

This project was a systems exercise as much as a robotics one: every design choice had to balance camera placement, reachability, grip geometry, and the messiness of real fruit.
